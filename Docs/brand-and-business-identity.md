# 2. Business Description

Maillot Store is a sports merchandise retailer specializing in football jerseys, basketball jerseys, and sports accessories. The business operates through a physical retail shop while serving customers both locally and internationally through digital channels.

The proposed Retail Management & E-commerce System will unify online sales, physical store operations, inventory management, influencer marketing, customer management, and reporting into one centralized platform.

---

# 3. Mission

To provide sports fans with authentic, high-quality jerseys and accessories through an exceptional shopping experience both online and in-store.

# 8. Customer Segments

### Guest Customers

Customers who wish to purchase products without creating an account.

### Registered Customers

Customers with accounts that allow them to:

- Track orders
- View order history
- Save delivery addresses
- Receive notifications
- Enjoy a faster checkout experience

### Influencers

Individuals approved by Maillot Store to promote products using referral links and earn commissions from successful sales.
